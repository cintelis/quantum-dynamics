How it works

  1. RankMatch what suppliers sell against thousands of published devices.
  2. DesignGenerate new spacer molecules where the catalogue runs out.
  3. SimulateModel how energy moves through the film, from a laptop to GPUs.
  4. TestPartner labs make the top picks; results feed the next ranking.
